About XwerseMovies

Last updated: November 2026

XwerseMovies, operated by Xwerse, is an online video-streaming service for viewers in India and around the world. Registered users can browse a catalogue of movies and TV shows and stream them for free in their browser, supported by advertisements. For Full HD quality and far fewer ads, users can choose Premium: a small one-time, pay-per-view fee for a specific movie, season or complete series — with no subscription.

What We Offer

  • Free streaming catalogue — browse, search, build My List, and resume where you left off.
  • Premium (pay per view) — pay once for Full HD access to a movie, a season, or a complete series, with very few ads and, where available, a download link, an embed link and Indian-language subtitles. Prices are in INR and there is no subscription.
