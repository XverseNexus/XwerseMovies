Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains cancellations and refunds for XwerseMovies' paid Request feature. Streaming the free catalogue has no payment and no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel there. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR).

1. One-Time Payments, No Subscription

Each Request — Movie ₹9, Season ₹29, Complete Web Series ₹59 — is a single, one-time charge for that Request. There is no recurring billing or renewal. You pay again only if you place another Request.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel a Request for a full refund any time before we start working on it (status shown as Pending in My Requests). To cancel, email vconic3@gmail.com with your registered email and payment reference/order ID. Once a Request is Accepted or Completed, it can no longer be cancelled, except as described in Section 3.

3. When You Get a Refund

  • Request rejected — if we reject your Request (for example the title cannot be arranged or cannot be provided lawfully), you get a full refund.
  • Not delivered in time — if your Request is not completed within a reasonable time after the estimated 24–72 hour window and we cannot resolve it with you, you get a full refund.
  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same Request because of a technical error. The extra amount is refunded.
  • Unauthorised transaction — a payment was made without your authorisation (subject to verification).
  • Delivered content not working — if the video we delivered cannot be played and we cannot fix it, you get a refund.

To claim a refund, contact us within 7 days of the transaction (or of the problem arising) on our Contact page or at vconic3@gmail.com, with your registered email and the payment reference/order ID.

A Request that has been Completed — the content is available to you and plays correctly — is not refundable, because the service has been delivered.

4. How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, or net banking) through Cashfree Payments. We start the refund within 3 business days of approval, and it usually reaches your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. Failed or Pending Payments

If a payment fails or stays "pending" and no Request is created, no money is kept by us. Any amount debited for a failed transaction is automatically reversed by Cashfree or your bank, usually within 5–7 business days. If it is not, send us your payment reference and we will follow up with Cashfree.

6. Contact

For any billing question or refund request, email vconic3@gmail.com or use the Contact page. We reply within 48 hours.
