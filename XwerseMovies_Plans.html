How Premium works
1. Pick a title and pay

Search the title, choose Movie / Season / Series, and pay securely with UPI through Cashfree. Use the same email you sign in with — we show it to you with a copy button.

2. Get access

Once the payment is confirmed it appears in My Purchases as Processing. Titles we have already prepared unlock automatically; others are added within 24–72 hours and you get a notification.

3. Watch, download or embed

Open My Purchases and tap Watch. When available you also get the download link and the embed link.

Is there a refund?

Yes — if we cannot arrange your title, you get a full refund. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Can I buy the same title twice?

No. Once a title is bought and paid, it stays on your account — you cannot (and do not need to) purchase it again.
