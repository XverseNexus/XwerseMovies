Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information XwerseMovies, operated by Xwerse ("we", "us"), collects, why, how it is protected, and the choices you have. It applies to the XwerseMovies website and its related pages.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile name and picture.
  • Viewing activity — titles you watched, My List, ratings, and Continue Watching data, used to power those features.
  • Request and payment records — the title you requested, request type and status, the amount, payment status, and the payment reference/order ID and email returned by Cashfree. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank credentials.
  • Technical data — basic device and browser information and approximate region, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Messages — what you send us when you contact support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and viewer profiles.
  • To provide Continue Watching, My List, and notifications about your Requests.
  • To process Request payments, deliver your Request, and handle refunds and support.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who We Share It With

Only the service providers needed to run XwerseMovies:

  • Supabase — database and authentication; stores your account and viewing data.
  • Cashfree Payments — payment processing, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google — only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and show metadata and posters; no personal account data is sent to TMDB.
  • Video hosting / player providers — videos are streamed from third-party hosts. When you play a video, your browser connects to that host, which receives technical data such as your IP address and may show ads or set its own cookies under its own policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser storage (local storage, session storage, and a service worker) to keep you signed in, remember preferences and playback position, and make pages load faster. You can clear these from your browser settings, but parts of the Service may then stop working correctly.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. Payment and Request records are kept as long as needed for refunds, accounting, and legal requirements. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we must keep by law.

6. Security

Account data is protected with Supabase authentication and Row Level Security so users can only read their own records, and all traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Payments are handled by Cashfree's PCI-DSS compliant systems. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You can update your account details, change your password, and delete viewer profiles from Settings at any time. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing vconic3@gmail.com from your registered email. We respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where Data Is Stored

Our providers (such as Supabase and Vercel) may store and process data on servers outside India. By using the Service you understand your data may be transferred there under those providers' safeguards.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be shown here with an updated date.

11. Contact

Questions or complaints about your data? Email vconic3@gmail.com or use the Contact page.
